Our Story

Varish Partners was built on a simple observation: most businesses don't fail their financial and tax obligations out of neglect — they fail them because good advice arrives too late, or too generic to act on. Founded by Varun Shenoy and Vishwanath Prabhu, professionals with over two decades of combined experience across tax, compliance, and corporate advisory, the firm set out to close that gap: advice that arrives before a decision is made, not after it's already cost something.

What started as tax and compliance support for individuals and small businesses grew, as our clients grew, into a full consultancy practice — covering business registration, GST, financial planning, fractional CFO support, and transaction advisory. The common thread has stayed the same throughout: we work alongside clients through the year, not just at deadlines, and we measure our work by whether it actually moved the client's business forward.

A Story That Defines How We Work

One engagement in particular captures what that looks like in practice. A client came to us for their overseas expansion — advice on the right investment strategy, and a way through the complexity of cross-border taxation and transfer pricing. The timelines were tight, so the team built a comprehensive proposal upfront, laying out exactly where we'd focus and why, then worked through the engagement with the same discipline: a concise final report, risks clearly categorised, delivered on schedule.

The result was concrete — the client moved into a new international market with an anticipated 20% increase in investment potential, and the risk mitigation work built into our advice is expected to cut their compliance costs by roughly 25%. It's one of many client stories, but it's a fair example of the standard we hold ourselves to: proactive planning, clear deliverables, and outcomes clients can actually measure.

Where we are today

Today, Varish Partners supports individuals, startups, and established businesses across tax consultancy, GST compliance, financial planning, and business advisory — the same client-first approach the firm was founded on, applied at a larger scale.
